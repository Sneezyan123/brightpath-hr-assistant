# Sick Leave Policy
Applies to employees of Brightpath Analytics in the Riga and Lisbon offices. Version 2.4, effective 1 January 2026.

## 1. Reporting sickness
If you are ill, tell your manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence and register the sick leave in the HR assistant the same day. Sick leave can be registered up to 7 calendar days after the first day of absence. Sick leave registered within these rules is approved automatically.

## 2. Paid sick days
Full-time and part-time employees have 15 paid sick days per calendar year at 100% of salary. After the 15 paid sick days are used, statutory sickness benefit applies: in Latvia it is paid by the State Social Insurance Agency, in Portugal by Social Security.

## 3. Doctor's note (medical certificate)
You can self-certify sick leave of up to 3 consecutive working days. From the 4th consecutive working day of sickness, a doctor's note (medical certificate) is required and must be uploaded to the HR portal within 5 working days.

## 4. Caring for a sick child
Employees may use up to 5 of their paid sick days per calendar year to care for a sick child under 14 years old. Register these days as sick leave and mention child care in the reason.

## 5. Return to work after long sickness
After a sickness absence longer than 10 working days, the employee and manager hold a short return-to-work conversation to agree on any adjustments such as reduced hours.

# Parental Leave Policy
Applies to employees of Brightpath Analytics in the Riga and Lisbon offices. Version 1.7, effective 1 January 2026.

## 1. Eligibility for parental leave
All full-time and part-time employees are eligible for company parental leave from their first day, for birth, adoption, or surrogacy. Contractors are not eligible for company parental leave.

## 2. Parental leave entitlement
The primary caregiver receives 16 weeks (80 working days) of fully paid company parental leave. The secondary caregiver receives 4 weeks (20 working days) of fully paid company parental leave. Part-time employees receive the same number of weeks, paid pro-rata to their FTE. Company parental leave must be taken within 12 months after the birth or adoption, and can be split into at most two blocks.

## 3. Relation to statutory parental benefits
Company parental leave runs at the same time as statutory maternity, paternity, or parental leave. During the company-paid weeks, Brightpath tops up the statutory benefit to 100% of the employee's salary.

## 4. Requesting parental leave
Request parental leave at least 8 weeks before the planned start date and state the expected date of birth or adoption. Every parental leave request needs manager approval and stays pending until the manager decides.

## 5. Phased return after parental leave
For the first 4 weeks after returning from parental leave, employees may work 80% of their hours while receiving 100% of their salary.

# Other Leave Types and Public Holidays
Applies to employees of Brightpath Analytics in the Riga and Lisbon offices. Version 2.0, effective 1 January 2026.

## 1. Bereavement leave
Employees receive 5 paid working days of bereavement leave on the death of an immediate family member (spouse or partner, child, parent, or sibling) and 2 paid working days on the death of another relative (grandparent, grandchild, parent-in-law, aunt, or uncle). Bereavement leave needs no notice and is approved automatically.

## 2. Unpaid leave
After the probation period, employees can request up to 30 working days of unpaid leave per calendar year. Unpaid leave must be requested at least 14 calendar days in advance and always needs manager approval. Health insurance continues during unpaid leave.

## 3. Study leave
After the probation period, employees receive 5 paid study leave days per calendar year for exams or courses related to their role. Study leave must be requested at least 14 calendar days in advance and is approved automatically when these conditions are met.

## 4. Public holidays by office
Public holidays depend on the office. The Riga office follows Latvian public holidays (for example 18 November, Proclamation Day of Latvia, and 24–26 December). The Lisbon office follows Portuguese national holidays (for example 1 December and 8 December) plus the Lisbon municipal holiday on 13 June (Saint Anthony's Day). The full calendar is on the intranet. Public holidays that fall within a leave period are not deducted from the leave balance.

## 5. Jury duty and civic duties
Employees receive paid leave for the time needed for jury duty, serving as a witness, or other legally required civic duties. Send a copy of the summons to HR.

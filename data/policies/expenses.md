# Expenses, Payroll and Internal Requests
How employees of Brightpath Analytics handle expenses, payroll, equipment, and HR paperwork. Version 3.0, effective 1 January 2026.

## 1. Expense claims
Submit expense claims in the expense tool within 60 days of the purchase, with a receipt. Approved expenses are reimbursed with the next monthly salary. Any single expense above EUR 500 needs manager approval before you buy it.

## 2. Business travel
Book business travel through the travel desk. Flights under 6 hours are economy class. The hotel limit is EUR 150 per night in Riga and Lisbon and EUR 200 per night in other capital cities. Daily allowances (per diem) follow local law.

## 3. Equipment and IT requests
Request a laptop, monitor, software, or access rights by opening a ticket with the IT Helpdesk. Standard laptops are replaced every 3 years. A lost or stolen laptop or phone must be reported to IT Security within 24 hours.

## 4. Salary payment and payslips
Salary is paid on the 25th of each month; if the 25th is not a working day, it is paid on the previous working day. Payslips are available in the HR portal. Send payroll questions to the payroll team via the HR portal.

## 5. Updating personal data and bank details
Update your address, bank account, or emergency contact in the HR portal within 5 working days of the change. Bank account changes made after the 15th of a month take effect from the next month's salary.

## 6. Employment certificate and letters
Request a certificate of employment (for example, for a bank, landlord, or visa application) in the HR portal. HR issues it within 3 working days.

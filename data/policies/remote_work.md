# Hybrid and Remote Work Policy
Applies to employees of Brightpath Analytics in Riga and Lisbon. Version 2.2, effective 1 January 2026.

## 1. Hybrid work model and office days
Brightpath works in a hybrid model. Employees work from the office at least 2 days per week; Tuesday and Thursday are team days when the whole team is in the office. The other days can be worked from home.

## 2. Home office setup budget
After the probation period, employees receive a one-off home office budget of EUR 500 for a desk, chair, monitor, or other equipment. Claim it through the expense tool with receipts. Items bought with the budget belong to the employee.

## 3. Core working hours
Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time, when everyone should be available for meetings. Outside core hours, employees organise their working time flexibly with their team.

## 4. Working from abroad
Employees may work from another country inside the EU/EEA for up to 4 weeks per calendar year with their manager's approval, requested at least 2 weeks in advance. Working from abroad for more than 4 weeks, or from any country outside the EU/EEA, needs a separate review by HR and Legal because of tax and social security rules; contact HR before making plans.

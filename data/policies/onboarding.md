# Onboarding Guide for New Employees
How Brightpath Analytics welcomes new employees in Riga and Lisbon. Version 1.3, effective 1 January 2026.

## 1. Before your first day
Your employment contract is signed electronically. The People team sends a welcome email 5 working days before your start date with your schedule, office address, and buddy's name. Your laptop is prepared by IT and handed over on your first day.

## 2. Your first day
On the first day, arrive at the office reception at 09:30. You receive your access badge and laptop, the IT Helpdesk sets up your accounts, and you meet your manager and your onboarding buddy for lunch.

## 3. Onboarding buddy programme
Every new joiner gets an onboarding buddy from another team for the first 3 months. The buddy answers everyday questions about tools, people, and how things work.

## 4. First 90 days: probation, check-ins, and mandatory training
The probation period lasts 90 calendar days, with check-in meetings with your manager at 30, 60, and 90 days. New joiners must complete the mandatory trainings within the first 14 days: security awareness, data protection (GDPR), and the code of conduct.

## 5. Documents to provide when you join
In the first week, upload these through the HR portal: a copy of your ID document, bank account details, tax information (Latvian personal code in Riga, NIF tax number in Lisbon), and an emergency contact.

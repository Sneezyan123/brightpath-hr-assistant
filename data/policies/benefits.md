# Employee Benefits
Benefits for employees of Brightpath Analytics in Riga and Lisbon. Version 4.0, effective 1 January 2026.

## 1. Who is eligible for benefits
Full-time and part-time employees are eligible for the company benefits in this document from their first day unless a section says otherwise. Contractors are not eligible for company benefits.

## 2. Health insurance
Riga office: employees get private health insurance from NordHealth from the first day, covering outpatient care, hospital care, and dental care up to EUR 300 per year; family members can be added at the employee's own cost. Lisbon office: employees get the LusoCare health plan, which covers the employee and their children at no cost; a spouse or partner can be added at 50% of the premium. Part-time employees get the same health insurance as full-time employees.

## 3. Pension contributions
Riga office: Brightpath contributes 3% of gross salary to a private pension fund (Latvian 3rd pillar) when the employee also contributes at least 2%. Lisbon office: Brightpath contributes 3% of gross salary to the company retirement savings plan (PPR) after the probation period.

## 4. Wellness allowance
Full-time employees receive a wellness allowance of EUR 400 per calendar year for gym memberships, sports, yoga, or mental health apps; part-time employees receive it pro-rata to their FTE. Claim the wellness allowance through the expense tool with a receipt.

## 5. Learning and development budget
Every employee has a learning budget of EUR 1,000 per calendar year for courses, certifications, conferences, and books. Items above EUR 300 need manager approval before purchase.

## 6. Meals
Lisbon office: employees receive a meal allowance of EUR 10.20 per working day, paid on a meal card. Riga office: lunch is provided free of charge in the office on Tuesdays and Thursdays (team days).

## 7. Employee assistance programme (counselling)
All employees and their household members can use the confidential employee assistance programme: up to 6 free counselling sessions per year with a licensed psychologist, online or in person.

# Annual Leave Policy
Applies to employees of Brightpath Analytics in the Riga and Lisbon offices. Version 3.1, effective 1 January 2026.

## 1. Scope and eligibility
The annual leave policy applies to full-time and part-time employees with an employment contract. Contractors and freelancers are not covered by this annual leave policy; their time off is governed by their own service agreement, and they should contact their agency or contract owner about time off.

## 2. Annual leave entitlement: how many vacation days you get
Full-time employees receive 25 working days of paid annual leave (vacation days) per calendar year. Part-time employees receive annual leave pro-rata to their FTE: for example, an employee working 0.6 FTE receives 15 days per year. The leave year is the calendar year. Public holidays are not deducted from annual leave. Annual leave accrues from the first day of employment.

## 3. Requesting annual leave and notice period
Annual leave is requested through the HR assistant or the HR portal. Requests of more than 3 working days must be submitted at least 10 calendar days before the first day of leave; shorter requests can be made with less notice. A request is approved automatically when the employee has enough remaining balance, the notice period is respected, and it does not overlap another request. Requests longer than 10 working days need manager approval and stay pending until the manager decides.

## 4. Annual leave during the probation period
The probation period is the first 90 calendar days of employment. Annual leave accrues during probation, but every annual leave request made during probation needs manager approval, regardless of its length.

## 5. Carry-over of unused vacation days
Up to 5 unused annual leave days can be carried over to the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March of the next year, otherwise they are forfeited. Unused annual leave is not paid out, except for the final settlement when employment ends.

## 6. Cancelling or changing annual leave
An employee can cancel a pending or approved leave request that has not started yet, through the HR assistant. The days are returned to the balance immediately. To change dates, cancel the request and submit a new one.
